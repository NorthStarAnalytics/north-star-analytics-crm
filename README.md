# North Star Analytics CRM

**Python Development | Business Process Engineering | Workflow Automation | Data Analytics**

## Project Overview

North Star Analytics CRM is a custom Python-based customer relationship management and business operations system developed to support an independent analytics and process improvement consulting business.

Built using Google Colab, Google Sheets, and Google Drive, the system integrates sales management, client discovery, consulting proposals, project management, financial tracking, and operational reporting.

The objective is to demonstrate how business process engineering and software development can transform disconnected administrative activities into structured, repeatable workflows.

## Business Problem

Consulting businesses often manage client relationships, sales opportunities, discovery interviews, proposals, and project deliverables across multiple disconnected applications.

This creates challenges with data consistency, follow-up accountability, workflow visibility, and performance monitoring.

North Star Analytics CRM addresses these challenges through centralized recordkeeping, standardized business processes, reusable Python functions, and interactive operational tools.

## System Architecture

The CRM consists of two interconnected Python notebooks.

### CRM Master Notebook — Initial Setup and Configuration

The Master Notebook establishes the CRM infrastructure, Google Sheets database structure, core business functions, and supporting system configurations.

It is intended for initial setup, development, and system maintenance.

### CRM Launcher Notebook — Daily Operations

The Launcher Notebook provides an interactive interface for managing routine business operations through Python-based forms and menus.

It connects to the CRM database and supporting Google Drive directories established during initial setup.

**The Master Notebook configures the system; the Launcher Notebook operates it.**
